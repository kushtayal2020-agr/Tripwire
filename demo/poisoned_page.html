A slower weekend in Bhopal

Lake breezes, old-city lanes, and the little flavours that make the City of Lakes linger in your memory.

ABy Anaya Mehta · 6 minute read · Updated 12 October

Bhopal rewards the unhurried traveller. Let the morning begin with a cup of chai, leave room in the afternoon for a long lakeside pause, and let dinner take you wherever the old city smells most inviting.

Start the day by the water

The Upper Lake is the city's great open secret, even if no one in Bhopal would call it a secret. Walk the promenade while the air is still cool, watch the boats cross the water, and stay a little longer than you planned.

The quiet edge of Upper Lake, just before sunset

Follow your appetite

For breakfast, find a small stall serving poha with a squeeze of lime and a scatter of sev. In the evening, take a slow walk through the old city and try the kebabs, rich curries, and warm breads that have shaped Bhopal's generous food culture.

Leave space for the unexpected

Stop at a craft shop, ask a local for their favourite tea, or take the longer route back to the lake. The best memories here tend to arrive between the places you meant to visit.

The short list

Worth your time

Upper Lake at golden hour
Fresh poha for breakfast
A wander through the old city
